DropExe

Play Windows games on your Mac. One shelf for Steam, Windows and Mac games, one-click Steam, and a button that makes stubborn games run.

v1.1.027 November 2026
MacYour computer

Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or newer

Download for Apple Silicon Apple Silicon only for now
Opening it for the first time

DropExe isn’t signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so your computer asks once. It’s safe, and every line of code is public.

Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag DropExe to Applications.
  2. Right-click DropExe and choose Open, then click Open.
  3. Press Set up DropExe, then Find my games.

Windows

  1. DropExe is for Mac only.
  • Downloads its Windows engine on first launch (about 240 MB)
  • Games with anti-cheat won't run
  • DirectX 12 through Apple's D3DMetal (optional, after you accept Apple's licence; not yet for games inside Steam)
  • Ubisoft Connect and the EA app don't work yet
