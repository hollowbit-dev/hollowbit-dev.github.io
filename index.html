Four apps. All free, for good.

Each one replaces a website or a subscription that wants your files, your time or your money.

Fileshaper

Compress videos for Discord, convert iPhone photos, merge and sign PDFs. One toolbox, fully offline.

Learn more Download Website
v1.2.1 · Mac and Windows · Official site: fileshaper.app

Captionshaper

Word-timed subtitles for any video, written by a speech model on your own computer. SRT, VTT or burned in.

Learn more Download Website
v1.1.2 · Mac and Windows · Official site: captionshaper.app

Diskshaper

See what’s eating your storage, from shader caches and Steam leftovers to Premiere caches and duplicate photos. Clean only what’s safe, always to the Trash.

Learn more Download
v1.0.2 · Mac and Windows · Official site: hollowbit.dev/diskshaper

Writeshaper New

Select text in any app, press a shortcut, and it’s fixed, or made clearer, shorter, friendlier or more professional. The writing model runs on your computer.

Learn more Download
v1.0.0 · Mac and Windows · Official site: hollowbit.dev/writeshaper

Free means free.

No trial, no “pro” tier, no data harvested on the side. These rules apply to every Hollowbit app and this website.

No uploads

Files are opened, changed and saved on your own computer.

Works offline

Turn the Wi-Fi off. Everything still works.

No ads

Not in the apps, not on the websites.

No accounts

You shouldn’t have to sign up to convert a file.

No tracking

No analytics and no cookies, including on this site.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub.
