The rating plate

Every Hollowbit app, and this website, is built to the same numbers. They don't change when a product gets popular.

  1. Bytes uploaded0Your files are opened, changed and saved on your computer. They work with the Wi-Fi off.
  2. Ads0Not in the apps, not on the websites.
  3. Accounts0You shouldn't have to sign up to convert a file or caption a video.
  4. Trackers0No analytics and no cookies, here or anywhere else Hollowbit runs.
  5. Watermarks0No trial, no “pro” tier, nothing stamped on your work.
  6. Closed code0Everything is on GitHub under the GPL. Read it, build it, change it.

Hi, I'm Elvis.

I'm Elvis Zygmanta, and I'm based in Thailand. Hollowbit is my studio for small, free tools that treat the people using them with respect.

It started with an annoyance: shrinking a video for Discord or opening an iPhone photo on Windows meant uploading it to a website, waiting in a queue and paying to remove a watermark. A normal computer can do those jobs in seconds. So each Hollowbit app takes one of those websites and does the job offline, for free, as polished as anything you'd pay for.

Each one ships with installers for Windows and Mac, its own website, and automated tests that drive the real app before every release.
