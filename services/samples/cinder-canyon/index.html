Good to know

What should I bring?

Closed-toe shoes with grip, sun protection and layers. We bring water, snacks and trekking poles.

What if the weather turns?

If it’s unsafe to hike, we’ll move your tour to another day or give you a full refund.

Do you offer private tours?

Yes. Private hikes for families, couples and small teams start at $320 for up to four people.

Is there a minimum age?

Each tour lists its minimum age. Under-16s must hike with an adult from their group.
