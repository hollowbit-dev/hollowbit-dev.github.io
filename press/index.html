Short description

Hollowbit Studio makes free apps for Mac and Windows that never upload your files. Each one replaces a website or subscription with an app that does the job on your own computer, with no ads, no accounts and no watermarks.

Long description

Hollowbit Studio is an independent studio run by Elvis Zygmanta. It started with a simple annoyance: everyday jobs like shrinking a video, converting a photo or adding subtitles meant uploading files to a website, waiting in a queue and paying to remove a watermark.

Each Hollowbit app takes one of those jobs and does it offline, for free. The line-up covers file conversion (Fileshaper), subtitles (Captionshaper), storage cleaning (Diskshaper), writing help (Writeshaper), PC speed-ups (Startshaper), photo editing (Lightshaper), short clips from long videos (Clipshaper), moving to a new computer (Moveshaper), making music (Melodyshaper) and app automations (Fuseduo).

Every app is open source, works with the Wi-Fi off and has no paid tier. There is no tracking in the apps or on the websites.

About the founder

Elvis Zygmanta is the founder of Hollowbit Studio. Elvis designs and releases the studio’s apps, websites and videos, and runs its community on Discord.

Select text in any app, press a shortcut, and it’s fixed, or made clearer, shorter, friendlier or more professional. The writing model runs on your computer.

Startshaper

Find out why your computer is slow. See every app that starts by itself, what it is in plain words, and switch off the ones you don’t need. Every change can be undone.
