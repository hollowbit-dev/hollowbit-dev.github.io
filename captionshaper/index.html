From raw video to captioned in a minute.

Word-timed

Whisper runs locally and times every word to the voice. A one-minute clip takes about ten seconds on an M-series Mac.

Accurate

0.7% word errors on our test clips. Music and silence are skipped, so captions don’t appear when nobody is talking.

Styled

Nine looks, from clean subtitles to TikTok-style word pop. What you see in the preview is what gets burned in.

Yours

Export SRT or VTT for YouTube and editors, or a finished video with the captions burned in. No watermark.

Captions without the subscription.

Free for Windows and Mac. No account, no watermark, nothing uploaded.
