Hi, I’m Elvis.

I’m Elvis Zygmanta, based in Thailand. Hollowbit is my studio for small, free tools that treat the people using them with respect.

It started with an annoyance. Shrinking a video for Discord or opening an iPhone photo on Windows meant uploading it somewhere, waiting in a queue and paying to remove a watermark, for jobs a normal computer can do in seconds. So each Hollowbit app takes one of those websites and does the job offline, for free.

Every app ships with installers for Windows and Mac, its own website, and automated tests that drive the real app before each release.

What Hollowbit sticks to

  1. No uploads.Files are opened, changed and saved on your computer. Everything works with the Wi-Fi off.
  2. No ads.Not in the apps, not on the websites.
  3. No accounts.You shouldn’t have to sign up to convert a file.
  4. No tracking.No analytics and no cookies, including on this site.
  5. No watermarks.No trial and no “pro” tier. The free version is the whole thing.
  6. Open source.Every line is on GitHub under the GPL.
