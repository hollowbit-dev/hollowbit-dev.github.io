Privacy.

Short version: Hollowbit doesn’t collect anything about you.

This website

hollowbit.dev sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. Fonts and images are served from this site. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

The apps

Fileshaper, Captionshaper, Diskshaper and Writeshaper work on your own computer. Your files are never uploaded. None of the apps have accounts, ads or analytics.

The apps only go online for things you can see: each app can check GitHub once a day for a new version (you can turn it off), and Captionshaper downloads its speech models the first time you pick one. Nothing about you or your files is sent.

Downloads

Installers are served by GitHub Releases, under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Email

If you email me, I use your address only to reply. Nobody gets added to a mailing list.

Anything unclear? Get in touch.
